# Aion Forge — Exemplary Universes

Version: 1.0

This document records universes that demonstrate the depth of emergence within Aion Forge.

Each entry was discovered through exploration, not authored.

The simulation generated these outcomes from simple rules applied over deep time.

---

## How to Use This Document

Enter any seed number into the seed field in Aion Forge and click **Generate**.

The universe described will appear exactly as recorded.

This is determinism at work.

Figures below are for the default parameters under simulation rules v5 (planets read from their climate histories, their air classed by the pressure it has today, and their orbits formed beyond their star's heat). Rules change between versions, so a seed's numbers are only exact for the version they were counted under. "Worlds with organisms" counts planets with microbial or more advanced life.

## The Abundant Universe

**Seed:** `271828`
**Galaxy Type:** Elliptical
**Character:** 748 worlds with organisms. 133 civilizations, the most of any seed here; 44 systems host one that got past farming.

An elliptical galaxy with unusually favorable conditions for life emergence.

The density of matter and favorable stellar distribution conspired to seed biology across an improbable number of worlds.

Many of these biospheres independently reached the dominant stage.

This is the most civilization-rich universe recorded here.

---

## The Classic Spiral

**Seed:** `100000`
**Galaxy Type:** Spiral
**Character:** 682 worlds with organisms. 97 civilizations.

A balanced, archetypal spiral universe.

Stars distributed across well-defined arms, biospheres taking hold across a wide range of stellar types.

Its civilizations rose independently, unaware of each other. Fewer than half got past farming. Some never had the chance: their worlds offer no fire, with no exposed land or air too thin to burn in.

A good universe for understanding Aion Forge's normal range.

---

## The Mathematical Spiral

**Seed:** `3141592`
**Galaxy Type:** Spiral
**Character:** 713 worlds with organisms. 113 civilizations.

A close cousin of seed `100000` in its statistics, but with different stars, different worlds, different stories.

Two universes with similar numbers but entirely different histories.

A demonstration of why seeds matter.

## The Elliptical Network

**Seed:** `404040`
**Galaxy Type:** Elliptical
**Character:** 725 worlds with organisms. 130 civilizations.

Elliptical galaxies are thought to be less favorable for life — less disk structure, more chaotic stellar distributions.

This universe proves that assumption wrong.

More worlds with organisms arose here than in either spiral above.

Emergence does not require perfect conditions.

---

## The Irregular Pioneer

**Seed:** `137035`
**Galaxy Type:** Irregular
**Character:** 726 worlds with organisms. 120 civilizations.

Irregular galaxies are the rarest galaxy type and the most chaotic.

That it holds more life than the classic spiral is unexpected.

This universe invites the question: does chaos create opportunity?

---

## The Former Sparse Witness

**Seed:** `161803`
**Galaxy Type:** Elliptical
**Character:** 711 worlds with organisms. 108 civilizations.

Under the first rules this was the quiet universe, kept here for contrast. Once planets were read from their climate histories (rules v3) it became as rich as any other.

With 2,000 stars, default parameters now give every seed a similar amount of life. A quiet universe comes from the laws instead: apply the **Fragile Life** preset, and about a sixth as many systems hold organisms.

Some universes age without any biology to witness them.

That outcome is as valid as any other.

## Experimental Configurations

These seeds become particularly interesting when laws are modified.

### Seed `13579` with Emergence Sensitivity 2.0

Life overwhelms the galaxy.

Adjust the slider under **Edit parameters…** and observe the transformation.

### Seed `9999` with Intelligence Modifier 0.1

Life spreads across 71 worlds in the sample, but intelligence struggles to emerge.

Biospheres flourish in silence.

### Seed `112358` with Entropy Rate 2.0

Stars age rapidly.

Civilizations race against dying suns.

## Using the Gallery

When you encounter a universe worth remembering:

1. Click **SAVE UNIVERSE** in the HUD.
2. It joins your **GALLERY**.
3. Use **↓** to export it as a `.json` file.
4. Share the file. Anyone who imports it will see the same universe.

Universes are portable artifacts.

Seeds are their fingerprints.
